
# ShashCode Progress Titles (Java + DSA Sheet)

This document explains how **titles (levels)** work on ShashCode, what they represent,
and **how they are unlocked**.  
There is **no fixed order** — you are free to solve topics in any sequence.

Titles are based on **coverage and depth**, not playlist order.

---

## 🏅 ShashCode Titles (Level 1 → 5)

1. **Novice**
2. **Coder**
3. **Problem Solver**
4. **Algorithmist**
5. **DSA Specialist**

Titles are **permanent once unlocked**.

## 🏆 Title Unlock Rules

Titles are unlocked when you **qualify** based on your progress.
You are **not required** to follow any fixed order.

---

### 🟢 Level 1 — Novice

**Purpose:** Entry identity

**Unlock when ANY ONE is true:**
- 5 subtopics completed (anywhere in the sheet)  
- OR 10 questions solved  

---

### 🔵 Level 2 — Coder

**Purpose:** Breadth across fundamentals

**Unlock when:**
- At least **1 subtopic completed** in **ANY 2 different buckets**  
  (from Bucket A, B, or E)

---

### 🟣 Level 3 — Problem Solver

**Purpose:** Pattern-based thinking

**Unlock when:**
- At least **1 subtopic completed** from **Bucket C (DSA Patterns)**  
- AND total subtopic completion ≥ **40%**  

---

### 🔴 Level 4 — Algorithmist

**Purpose:** Advanced algorithmic maturity

**Unlock when:**
- At least **1 subtopic completed** from **Bucket D (Advanced Algorithms)**  
- AND total subtopic completion ≥ **60%**  

---

### 🏆 Level 5 — DSA Specialist

**Purpose:** Full-spectrum DSA capability

**Unlock when ALL are true:**
- Total subtopic completion ≥ **85–90%**  
- At least **1 completed subtopic** in **ALL 5 buckets (A → E)**  

---

## ℹ️ Important Notes

- Topics are **never locked**
- You may solve **any topic in any order**
- Titles reflect **overall readiness**, not sequence
- Progress is tracked automatically
